Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

SonicSpeed Pro is published by TinyStack Labs. We believe private tools should remain private. SonicSpeed Pro is engineered as a 100% on-device video accelerator and audio DSP utility.

1. Zero Audio or Video Collection

SonicSpeed Pro processes video playback speed, audio amplification gain, vocal clarity filters, and silence detection strictly inside your browser's local sandbox via HTML5 and Web Audio API. No audio streams, video frames, tab history, or user browsing activity are ever recorded, stored remotely, or transmitted to any server.

2. Local Storage Usage

The extension utilizes Chrome's local storage API exclusively on your device to persist:

  • Your chosen playback velocity and volume preferences.
  • Your Pro license activation status and key.
  • Your cumulative real-time time saved statistics.

This data never leaves your machine.

3. License & Payment Processing

Payment transactions and license key fulfillments are securely managed by Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record). TinyStack Labs never receives or handles your credit card or billing credentials.

4. Third-Party Trackers

SonicSpeed Pro contains zero third-party analytics, tracking scripts, ad networks, or telemetry libraries.
